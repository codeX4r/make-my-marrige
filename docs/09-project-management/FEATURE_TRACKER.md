## Interpretation notes

- `VERIFIED_UNIT` for F-001 through F-006 means the local unit suite passed; it
  does not assert every behavior or acceptance criterion is covered.
- F-007's unit status is limited evidence for some authentication paths. It
  does not close the listed email, rate-limit, or production-security gaps.
- `BLOCKED` can coexist with persistence or design groundwork. It means the
  full tracked behavior cannot be safely completed without resolving the listed
  prerequisite.
- The dashboard, mockups, source folders, and OpenAPI operations are not counted
  as implemented product workflows.
